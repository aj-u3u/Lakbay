Google Maps resolved **Agriya Naturetainment** to the parent **Agriya** place.
It resolved **Tubalan Cove** to a different listing named **Maldives tubalan
malita davao occidental**; the 110 km driving and 108 km walking results for
that listing are deliberately omitted above. **Dahican Beach** is a coastline
rather than one confirmed public entrance. **Aliwagwag Falls Ecopark** still
needs a current reopening confirmation. None of these Maps results confirms
an exact visitor arrival pin.

Google Maps showed **Cycling not available** for all 18 origin-destination
pairs. Its separate **Two-wheeler** option is for a motorized vehicle and is
not a bicycle route. The transit view supplied bus-and-walk itineraries for
six places, but did not display a total bus distance in kilometres. Failure to
calculate a transit route does not establish that no local bus service exists.

These are single-origin observations. Route optimization needs travel costs
between *every selected pair of stops*, and a verified arrival point for any
route offered as traveler directions. Do not put these snapshot distances into
the destination table as permanent distances or use them as the optimizer's
pairwise matrix.
