# Davao Region Google Maps destination review

Reviewed 29 October 2026. The [18-row CSV](davao_region_google_maps_review_2026-10-29.csv) is a **curated research subset**, with three entries each for Davao City, Davao del Norte, Davao del Sur, Davao de Oro, Davao Occidental, and Davao Oriental. It adds direct Google Maps listing checks and current access evidence to the existing [75-row supported-place candidate file](davao_region_supported_place_candidates.csv). It is not a seed import or a list of destinations certified open for a particular travel date.

`maps_match` means the Maps result was opened and its displayed name and area compared with a separate operator or government source. `matched_broad_area` means the map pin is an island or beach, not a single visitor entrance. `related_venue_only` means Maps did not establish the proposed destination itself. Google Maps hours and its “Open” label are leads, not proof of admission or safe access. The URLs in `maps_lookup_url` are the searches used for this review; they are not arrival waypoints. All `route_ready` values are `no` because public gates, parking, piers, or trailheads and the last road approach have not been verified.

`access_evidence_status` describes the strongest evidence found. `not_confirmed_current` is **unknown**. `review_for_catalog` means the identity and a plausible current access lead are supported, but date-specific access and visitor arrival still need checking. `hold_*` records should stay out of traveler recommendations until the stated gap is resolved. The CSV leaves prices and hours out because they vary or need date-specific confirmation.

## Findings that change the catalog decision

- **Aliwagwag Falls Ecopark:** Google Maps showed open hours. [February 2026 reporting](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/amp/story/davao/davao-oriental-plans-aliwagwag-falls-reopening) says the park had closed after the October 2025 earthquakes and officials were assessing a reopening. No later official reopening notice was verified; hold park admission claims. The falls may still be visible from the road bridge, which is a different visit.
- **Museo Dabawenyo:** The [Maps listing](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Museo+Dabawenyo+Davao+City) and the [relocation report](https://pia.gov.ph/news/museo-dabawenyo-reopens-in-new-location/) point to Mabini Street. Do not reuse the former Pichon Street point. The [2026 city citizen charter](https://davaocity.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CGD_CC2026_VOLUMEIIE.pdf) lists public museum tours.
- **Aqua Water Park:** The [operator's page](https://www.landcolifestyleventures.com/day-tour/aqua-water-park/) says both “Open Daily” and “Close every Tuesdays.” Its [2026 weekday pass](https://onlineshopping.aquaria.landcolifestyleventures.com/?ID=413&page=product&val1=aqua-water-park-samal-islanddavao-2026-day-tour-pass-for-adults-weekdays) is on sale, but the date, price, amenity access, and Tuesday schedule should be checked at booking.
- **Davao Occidental:** [Little Boracay](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Little+Boracay+Beach+Resort+Santa+Maria+Davao+Occidental) is in **Santa Maria**, not Malita. The [provincial lodging list](https://davaooccidental.gov.ph/hotel-and-resort/) places visitor accommodation on Balut Island, but does not establish a current boat schedule. A road record names Tubalan Cove, while the Maps search opens a different private venue; hold that broad cove entry.
- **Other access leads:** The [Philippine Eagle Foundation](https://www.philippineeaglefoundation.org/book-online) offers a regular tour; [People's Park city tourism page](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/explore-the-city/attractions/man-made-nature-parks/peoples-park/) lists public hours and free entry; [PIA reported Subangan Museum reopened](https://pia.gov.ph/news/museums-in-davao-region-reopen/) on 12 January 2026. These sources still do not guarantee access on a chosen date.

For the complete regional candidate pool, keep using the [original audit](lakbay_dataset_location_audit.md), [arrival review](lakbay_dataset_arrival_verification.md), and [candidate README](davao_region_destination_candidates_README.md). Before database import, confirm each retained venue with its operator or responsible tourism office, record the confirmation date, and check a safe visitor arrival point. This review changed only documentation data; no seed, API, model, migration, or database was changed.
