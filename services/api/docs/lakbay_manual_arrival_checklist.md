# Lakbay+ manual arrival-point checklist

Use with the [118-row arrival review](lakbay_dataset_arrival_verification.md) and [place audit](lakbay_dataset_location_audit.md). PDF row numbers restart in each section. This list is for recording the point at which a traveler should **arrive**, not the center of an attraction. Check a box only after obtaining a usable Google Maps link or decimal coordinates and checking the last road approach. Keep the source and date beside the coordinate in your working records.

**Question to send a venue:** “For a visitor arriving by car, can you share the Google Maps pin for your public entrance, reception, parking, or drop-off? Is that entrance currently open?” For a pedestrian-only site, ask for the public walking entrance.

## Boat departure and landing confirmations

Ask for **two points**: where the traveler boards and where the boat lands. Confirm the operator, schedules/booking, and whether the landing is public. These are distinct from a road gate.

- [ ] Davao Oriental #10 San Victor Island — departure and island landing.
- [ ] Davao Oriental #15 Waniban Island — departure and island landing.
- [ ] Davao de Oro #17 Kopiat Island — departure in Mabini and island landing.

Several other rows may need a boat point, but first require identity or public-access confirmation: Panabo Mariculture Park, Asuncion River Cruise, Poo Kinablangan Sandbar, Oakwood/Oak Island, Balut Fish Port, and Sarangani Island. The [arrival review](lakbay_dataset_arrival_verification.md) explains those holds and the other broad or ambiguous places.

For each completed checkbox, record: **Google Maps link or decimal latitude/longitude; arrival type; confirming operator/office; confirmation date; and a note on the final road, walking, or boat leg.** No backend code or records were changed to make this checklist.
