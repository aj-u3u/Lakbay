# Archived dated reports

These dated audits, catalog reviews and datasets record how the Davao Region
place data and early readiness checks were reviewed between 29 October and
9 November 2026. They are kept as evidence for the manuscript and for future
catalog work, but they are historical: their counts, statuses and next steps
may be out of date.

For current status, read [core_focus.md](../core_focus.md), the
[handoff audit](../claude_codex_objectives_audit_2026-11-10.md) and the
[objectives plan](../objectives_panel_upgrade_plan_2026-11-09.md).

`scripts/stage_osm_destination_candidates.py` reads its default input,
`davao_region_osm_catalog_triage_2026-11-04.jsonl`, from this folder.
