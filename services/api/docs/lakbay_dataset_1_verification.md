**Meaning of status:** *Supported* means an independent source corroborates the place in the stated city/municipality. *Partial* means a related place is corroborated but the exact name or claim needs correction. *Unconfirmed* means adequate independent corroboration was not found. A supported place is **not** an approved routing waypoint. All visitor entrances/drop-offs still need a road-access check.

## Import and routing decision

1. **Do not use any coordinate in this PDF as a final arrival waypoint yet.** Agriya and the Panabo central park/mangrove pins are plausible *place locations*; the seawall point is contradicted; Maria Clara and BeachBuns have unverified access points. Carmen and Tagum rows have no coordinates at all.
2. **Correct structural errors first:** delete duplicate Museo Panabo and three empty Tagum rows; renumber Carmen 1, 2, 9, 10 after selecting the final places; match Tagum Freedom Park, Energy Park, and Ising memorial against the existing 118-place set before creating records.
3. **Keep time, admission, and activities as unverified unless the venue/operator or responsible local government confirms them.** Directory listings may be useful leads, but conditions change. For cafés/restaurants, a `₱0` entrance fee is misleading if the service involves paid food.
4. For each retained place, collect a Google Maps link or decimal coordinates for the **visitor arrival point** (public entrance, reception, parking, or safe drop-off), source, verification date, and whether the last road segment is publicly accessible. This is the point the routing service should use.
